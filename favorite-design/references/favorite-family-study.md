# FAVORITE design families

Read this reference when selecting an art direction or deciding whether a requested adaptation has become a fixed visual template.

## Scope and evidence

The official [product catalog](https://www.favo-soft.jp/soft/product.html) exposed 24 product and console-port entries when reviewed on 2026-10-12. The research pass retrieved every listed landing document, same-site styles and scripts where declared, 31 linked pages and four nested primary frames. The user-specified [WORLD page](https://dramaticcreate.com/world_re/world.html) adds a 25th primary document. Some entries are bundles or remasters of the same game; they are not 24 unrelated visual languages.

This is a bounded survey of the current catalog, with deeper work on the three user-specified sites and the designs below. It is not a claim that every historic campaign, subpage, platform edition, hover state or Flash animation was studied. Recorded actions establish timing; original source establishes assets and code; screenshots establish the visible composition at a particular viewport. Keep these evidence levels separate.

Original assets stay in the project research archive. This reference distributes links and design reasoning, not a game-art collection.

## How to synthesize a new direction

Choose the relationship between art, prose and interaction from the actual content. A character encounter, a location diary and a product catalog deserve different compositions even when they share type, color and motion rules.

1. Name the emotional direction and the content that must carry it. Identify which existing design decisions the user wants retained.
2. Select the principles that serve this content, and explain each choice before developing a project-specific composition. Do not force a fixed number of influences, a whole section order, a material sheet or a fixed palette merely because it came from FAVORITE.
3. Define stable geometry for recurring navigation, theme logos, text entrances and portrait stages before adding transitions. Changing content must not nudge common UI.
4. Give decoration a clear layer and role. A complete feather under text, a shoreline behind a person, and particles in the foreground are different elements. Avoid fixing a wrong layer by cutting the art into a narrow strip.
5. Use original motion as a relationship: background before phrase, phrase before logo; information before portrait; sparse foreground motion against a stable reading surface. Develop fresh timing and paths for the project.

The useful freedom is in composition, responsive behavior, typography, meaningful transitions and contextual interactions. The work's canon, the ownership of source art, and a user's explicitly retained custom cursor are not free variables.

## Original art and complete decoration

These are separate requirements: acquire an authentic source, then compose it without damaging the element the reader is meant to see.

- **Source fidelity:** use the official or verified original game image for a canon place or person when that is the project's requirement. Record its origin. An original abstract UI diagram or a new layout can still be created; do not present a newly drawn location as recovered game art.
- **Complete decorative element:** retain the visible contour of a feather, flower or ornament. Place it below live text, give it the space its silhouette needs, and set decorative layers to ignore pointer events. A short clipping window is not a fix for a wrong stacking order.
- **Sprite separation:** cropping one complete state out of a sprite sheet is valid extraction. It differs from cutting off the ends of that state's feather. Keep the full intended state; do not accidentally carry neighboring states into the new UI.
- **Scene framing:** a large environment may be framed responsively when its narrative landmarks and reading area remain legible. A requested full-body encounter must preserve the entire person, including feet. There is no universal `cover` or `contain` choice: decide for the image's role and verify the resulting visible bounds.
- **Independent art direction:** the ornament's purpose may be a link response, a chapter seam or ambient motion. Do not assign the same source graphic to all three roles automatically. Develop new spacing, paths and composition that fit each role.

## Project lessons that transfer

- A larger original KV can still be wrong when the user prefers a place-and-person composition. More official artwork is not automatically a better solution.
- An atmospheric header should occupy the same physical box while its background blends with the scene. A blank strip above the art cannot be repaired by stronger shadows.
- When a separator is an illustration, keep the full transparent image below foreground text/content, with enough layer space. `background-size: cover` inside a short mask can amputate the visible feathers.
- Use full-body art when that is the user's intended encounter. Check the nontransparent pixel bounds and stage height; a large square promotional PNG may contain only a half portrait.
- Voice discovery and voice UI are separate decisions. A small verified sample set should trigger further source investigation before reducing the entire design to a speaker button. Attribute clips to their exact game, character and scene. A silent protagonist must not inherit another person's line.
- A timed theme cycle is a project requirement here. Its requested five-second cadence and absence of selectors should not become a universal skill default.
- Brand-inspired direction is compatible with original layout. Reusing the same seam, paper box and avatar row everywhere can become as repetitive as a generic template.

## Source art and a public skill

Use direct original URLs to inspect art and source. Keep URL, resolved URL, retrieval date, dimensions and hash in the project manifest. A recording frame is motion evidence, not an asset source.

Use [source-evidence](source-evidence.md) to distinguish original files, clean page screenshots and recording observations. A page screenshot documents a particular composition; it is not a transparent asset suitable for reuse and does not establish the implementation of motion.

Before publishing the skill, separate reusable instruction/code from third-party game artwork, audio, extracted script text and website source. The [FAVORITE guideline](https://www.favo-soft.jp/soft/guideline.html) gives different rules for noncommercial, nonspoiler game screenshots and original game data; the [ENTERGRAM page](https://www.entergram.co.jp/sakuramoyu/) also states a reproduction restriction. Link to sources and create original explanatory diagrams for a redistributable package. Do not describe all official material as freely licensed because the project is educational.
